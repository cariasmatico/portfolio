ZOOM

Year
2025
Medium
Clarinet, live electronics and video
Tags
Multimedia, Electroacoustic
Performer
Adrián Sandi, clarinet
Premiere
Universidad Católica de Chile, 2025
Context
Interdisciplinary Composition Workshop, organised by Miguel Farías and Rodrigo Cádiz

ZOOM explores the act of instrumental performance by taking a close look at the physical and visual aspects of sound production. The camera is pointed at different parts of the performer, framing the clarinettist’s actions through radical changes of scale in the image.

At the same time, both the video and the sound are gradually transformed by digital means, blurring the line between human performance and electronic sound production.
