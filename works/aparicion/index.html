APARICIÓN

Year
2026
Medium
Three performers, live video, real-time 3D and camera tracking
Tags
Multimedia, Performance
Performed
Multimedia Composition concert, Hamburg (Feb 2026)
Blurred Edges Festival, Hamburg (Jun 2026)
Stimme X Festival, Hamburg (Jun 2026)

In APARICIÓN, three performers build and explore a world existing simultaneously in physical and digital realms. Through microphones, live video and motion tracking for camera movement, the performers attempt to access what lies beyond life. The mechanics of the piece are deliberately exposed: the audience can watch how the camera works and how microphones focus on the objects they amplify.

Over the course of the piece, the performers build an everyday scene from an old house: a rug, a wooden chair, a lamp and a series of small ceramic objects. As the performance unfolds, a spectral presence begins to manifest. The on-screen world drifts away from the stage, objects seem to move of their own accord, and sounds become dislocated from their sources.

APARICIÓN operates as a polyphony of interacting stage elements. At times the audience sees a genuine live video feed; at others, a 3D world appears with startling realism. Sound design and stage lighting occasionally take precedence and become the primary focus.
