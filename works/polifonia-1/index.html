Polifonía 1

Year
2025
Medium
Composed theatre for two speakers, recorded voices and electronics
Tags
Performance, Electroacoustic, Composition
Performed
“Intrafonías”, organised by Anilla MAC, Museum of Contemporary Art, Santiago de Chile

In Polifonía 1, a conversation intertwines with Bach’s Prelude No. 8 in E-flat major. In this intersection, the polyphony of the music extends into the text, giving new literary and poetic meanings to the original conversation. The dialogue unfolds on several levels: live speakers, recorded voices, transformed voices and fully electroacoustic sound. Through its three variations, the piece brings forth a third person in the conversation, referenced in the text. A soft, ghostly humming acts as a bridge between Baroque harmony and the dialogue between the two speakers.

Polifonía 1 is the first in a series of music-theatre pieces exploring the relationship between text, musicality and stage elements. By using scores not only for music but also for text, action, rhythm and space, the work translates the parameters of musical composition into the performative and scenic realm, treating all materials as musical materials.
