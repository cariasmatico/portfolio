Dispositivo 1: Asiento por favor

Year
2023
Medium
Interactive sound installation built from a chair
Tags
Installation, Electroacoustic
Exhibited
Museum of Contemporary Art, Parque Forestal, Santiago, as part of “Efímera_” by Anilla MAC (three days)
Performances
Consuelo Robledo

Dispositivo 1: Asiento por favor is a sound installation built around an acoustic and electroacoustic instrument made from an everyday object: a chair. Visitors are invited to engage with the piece, exploring its logic (or lack thereof) while affecting the sound environment of the space through their actions.

The work brings out the sonic qualities of everyday objects through amplification, transformation and estrangement. The sound of the chair is distorted as it travels through the space, enveloping the audience and changing the soundscape as the interactions with the object vary.

Alongside the exhibition, Consuelo Robledo gave two performances using the installation as an instrument for a composition of movement and sound.
