STREAMING_01

Year
2020
Medium
Performer, webcam and interactive Max/MSP patch, with text score
Tags
Multimedia, Electroacoustic, Performance

Composed during the global pandemic, STREAMING_01 challenges the notions of gaze and surveillance in digital communication. Using the device’s camera, the piece creates a tension between the performer’s presence and absence by transforming their voice and image through the digital distortion and saturation inherent to the medium.

The performer’s identity shifts between clarity and concealment until the climax, where their image disappears and only the voice remains. Meanwhile, the program autonomously generates a text as a conclusion and synthesis of the piece.

The piece was programmed in Max/MSP and comes with a text score, which the performer recites while pressing keys that trigger effects, electroacoustic clips and other events.
