Ejercicios de respiración

Year
2021
Medium
Oboe without reed
Tags
Composition
Commission
José Luis Urquieta, oboe
Premiere
Festival MusicAhora, La Serena, 2021
English title
Breathing Exercises

This piece explores the aeolian sonorities of the oboe played without a reed, using breathing techniques, vocal modulations, articulations, pronunciations and dynamics to build a musical discourse from this unconventional use of the instrument.

Ejercicios de respiración is both a study of this approach and a meditative piece that pushes interpretation towards the body. The intensity and frequency of the performer’s breathing are treated as musical material, creating moments of tension and release in the music and in the act of breathing itself. This demands the performer’s full presence and physical effort.
