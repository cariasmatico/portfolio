ANIMACIÓN

Year
2026
Medium
Dancer, string quartet, real-time 3D and motion tracking
Tags
Multimedia, Performance, Composition
Performed
Multimedia Composition concert, Hamburg (Jul 2026)
Status
Work in progress

Imagine a bottle. A glass bottle. The bottle is just sitting there, still. Suddenly, the bottle moves. It wiggles. It dances to a rhythm. The bottle is animated. It lives. The bottle is kind of cute. The bottle is our friend.

Imagine a body. A human body. The body is breathing, slowly. Suddenly, the breathing stops. The body falls to the ground. The body is dragged away. It ragdolls. Nothing is cute anymore. No more humanity.

ANIMACIÓN investigates the mechanisms by which the essence of the human form, or humanity itself, is diminished or stripped away. Through live motion tracking applied interchangeably to unexpected inanimate objects and live human avatars, the work explores how empathy develops, grows and dissolves. This connection is presented as an incomplete link, full of tensions and glitches, in which the transference of life is neither clean nor perfect.
