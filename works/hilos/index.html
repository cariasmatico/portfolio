Hilos

Year
2021
Medium
Alto saxophone
Tags
Composition
Premiere
David Sánchez, XXX Festival de Música Contemporánea UC, Santiago, 2021

Hilos explores the techniques, textures and speeds of the alto saxophone through the metaphor of a thread: a strand that moves back and forth, passing through a single point that, over time, connects fragments of different materials, creating a bridge where there was once emptiness. The music accelerates and slows down, following the impulses of its materials: at times anxious and bursting with energy, at others calm, with an almost imperceptible development.

Meanwhile, an arpeggio gradually takes shape as the thread weaves through each material, establishing a harmony note by note that later appears in a more transparent form.
