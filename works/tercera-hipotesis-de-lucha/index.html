Tercera Hipótesis de Lucha

Year
2021
Medium
Ensemble, conductor, electronics and video
Tags
Multimedia, Electroacoustic, Composition
Performers
Ensamble Palimpsesto, first concert of the 2021 virtual season
English title
Third Fight Hypothesis

Tercera Hipótesis de Lucha challenges the traditional functioning of an ensemble in the virtual realm, using the conductor as another performer and the voices and bodies of the performers as musical material. It also asks questions about the image of the person performing a piece, and how the viewer’s gaze constructs that event.

The piece presents a series of sound and visual landscapes made with electronics and video editing. The initial text becomes rhythmic material that gradually transforms in meaning, while the video builds these processes through different arrangements of the performers in space.

The electronics and video can only be completed once the performers have recorded their parts, which gives the piece a recursive, self-contained character. Video cues in the score guide possible interpretations.
